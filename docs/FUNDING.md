# Funding

Ferry is free and will stay free: no paid version, no ads, no telemetry, no data for sale.

## Current state

Ferry receives **no money** and has **no donation account** yet. The maintainer pays the little it
costs (nothing so far: GitHub hosts the code, CI and the website for free).

## When donations open

Donations will go through platforms that show every payment publicly:

- [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com): every income and every expense, with its receipt,
  is listed on the project's page.
- [Liberapay](https://liberapay.com): recurring donations; the total income is shown publicly.

Their links will then appear here and in the *Sponsor* button of the repository
(`.github/FUNDING.yml`). Until then, any request for money in Ferry's name is not from the project.

## Rules

- **Money buys nothing in the project.** Donations and sponsors do not get features, priority,
  placement or a say in decisions ([GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md)).
- **What the money is for**, in this order: security audits and their fixes, code signing that is
  not free, devices for testing (for example phones with aggressive battery savers), a domain for the
  website. Paying the maintainer's own time is not planned; if it ever is, it will be announced
  here first.
- **Grants** (for example from NLnet) are listed below with their amount, their purpose and a link
  to the public announcement.
- **Once a year**, and whenever something changes, this page lists the income and spending of the
  past year.
